A QUICK TRIP AROUND THE BASES

Aim. Swing. Bring it home.

When you bat

Aim at the ball and click once to swing. After a hit, click first base to run. Select any runner, then choose a base. They stop when they arrive.

When you defend

Choose a pitch. Aim inside the box, hold the mouse, then release near the green. Fielders catch automatically. Click a base to throw.

The score

Three strikes end a batter’s turn. Four balls award first base. Three outs switch sides. Play three innings, then extra innings if tied.

A few friendly rules

A force out happens when a runner must reach the next base. On a caught fly, return to the original base before advancing: that’s tagging up.

After 8 seconds without progress, a 5-second warning appears. Runners still between bases are then out. No steals or bunts.
